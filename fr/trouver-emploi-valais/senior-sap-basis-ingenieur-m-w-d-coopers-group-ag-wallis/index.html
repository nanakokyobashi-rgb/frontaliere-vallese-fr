Description complète

For our client, we are looking for an experienced Senior SAP Basis Engineer to join the IT organization in a global enterprise environment. In this role, you will be responsible for the design, implementation, operation, security, and continuous improvement of the SAP landscape, ensuring the stability, performance, availability, and compliance of mission-critical SAP systems across SAP Private Cloud and on-premises environments.

Working closely with SAP, infrastructure, application, security teams, and external service providers, you will provide technical leadership for SAP infrastructure initiatives, upgrades, migrations, and cloud transformations. In addition, you will drive operational excellence through proactive monitoring, performance optimization, automation, and continuous improvement initiatives, while ensuring compliance with security standards, regulatory requirements, and business objectives.

Start date: 30 Nov 2026

Planed Durration: 29 Nov 2027

Workload: 100%

Contract Type: Contracting

Location: Visp

Remote: Yes, one day/week

Your tasks

  • Ensure high availability, stability, and performance of SAP landscapes including S/4HANA, ECC, BW, Solution Manager, SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), SAP Router, Web Dispatcher, and related technologies.
  • Lead technical troubleshooting and resolution of complex SAP Basis incidents and performance issues.
  • Drive proactive monitoring, capacity planning, and performance optimization activities.
  • Ensure compliance with defined service levels (SLAs), operational KPIs, and availability targets.
  • Coordinate disaster recovery testing, business continuity activities, and system resilience improvements.

SAP Architecture & Engineering

  • Design and maintain SAP system architectures aligned with enterprise standards and business requirements.
  • Lead SAP installation, upgrades, migrations, refreshes, Unicode conversions, and technical conversions.
  • Provide technical expertise for S/4HANA transformations, cloud migrations, and strategic SAP initiatives.
  • Define SAP infrastructure standards, lifecycle management strategies, and operational best practices.
  • Evaluate new SAP technologies and coordinate proof-of-concept activities.

Security & Compliance

  • Ensure SAP platforms comply with company security policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Coordinate implementation of SAP Security Notes, kernel patches, and operating system updates.
  • Support audits and validation activities related to GxP, SOX, and cybersecurity controls.
  • Drive vulnerability remediation and security hardening activities across the SAP landscape.
  • Ensure proper technical documentation and compliance evidence is maintained.

Cloud & SAP Private Cloud Operations

  • Act as technical counterpart for SAP and managed service providers in SAP Private Cloud environments.
  • Coordinate SAP Cloud ALM, Solution Manager, and monitoring integrations.
  • Support SAP BTP integrations and cloud-based SAP services.
  • Manage relationships with SAP support teams and cloud service providers to ensure operational excellence.

Automation & Continuous Improvement

  • Identify opportunities for automation of SAP operational activities.
  • Develop and improve automated monitoring, reporting, deployment, and maintenance processes.
  • Implement best practices for operational efficiency and service quality.
  • Drive continuous service improvement initiatives based on operational metrics and business needs.

Vendor & Service Management

  • Provide technical governance and oversight of outsourced service providers.
  • Review service delivery quality, operational performance, and contractual commitments.
  • Support transition, transformation, and optimization initiatives involving external partners.
  • Ensure adherence to operational processes, standards, and change management requirements.

Project Leadership

  • Serve as SAP Basis technical lead for infrastructure and application projects.
  • Coordinate technical activities across multiple teams and suppliers.
  • Participate in project planning, risk management, cutover execution, and post-go-live support.
  • Provide technical guidance and mentoring to junior SAP Basis engineers.

Your Profile

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience.

Experience

  • 8+ years of SAP Basis administration and engineering experience.
  • Strong experience supporting large enterprise SAP landscapes.
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA and HANA databases.
  • Experience with SAP Private Cloud Edition (PCE) environments.
  • Proven experience managing outsourced SAP operational services.
  • Experience leading SAP technical projects and migrations.
